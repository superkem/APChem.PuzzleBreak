How points work

Word search

  • Each word found: Easy 5, Medium 7, Hard 9
  • All words found: bonus of 15, 20 or 30
  • Reveal one word from its clue: −4
  • Show the whole word list: −25 (once per grid)
  • Hint: −3

Crossword

  • Each word finished: Short 7, Standard 8, Long 9
  • Whole puzzle finished: bonus of 15% of the word points
  • Check that finds a wrong letter: −2
  • Hint: −3

Scramble

  • Each word: Easy 10, Medium 15, Hard 20
  • Each wrong try takes 2 points off that word
  • Hint: −4

Hangman

  • Each word: Easy 11, Medium 15, Hard 19
  • Plus 1 for every wrong guess you did not use
  • Clue hidden: +4 a word
  • Hint: −4

A middle-level game with no help is worth about 120 points. Harder application clues give 3 extra points when you solve them. Harder settings pay more, and hints cost more than they did in the biology set. Points can go below zero, so use hints and reveals carefully.
